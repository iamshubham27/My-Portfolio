about

Hi, my name is Shubham.

I’m a B.Tech Computer Science student at Government Engineering College, Jhalawar, in the 2023–2027 batch. I’m genuinely curious about how software actually gets used — not just how it’s architected — which is why most of what I build ends up being a complete, working application rather than a scoped-down exercise.

My focus sits across Python development, web development, and software engineering more broadly — backend logic, databases, and the interfaces people actually touch. I’ve done internships in web development and Python, and outside of coursework I keep building: a desktop card-game scorer, a full sports-management platform for my college, e-commerce UI practice, and a couple of Flask-based web apps.

I’m equally drawn to UI/UX and automation — I’d rather spend an extra hour making an interface feel considered, or a workflow disappear, than ship something merely functional. New frameworks, new problem spaces, and the occasional dependency error at 1am are honestly what keep it interesting.

Away from the keyboard, I play volleyball and table tennis, and spend time on creative editing with Adobe Premiere Pro, Photoshop, and DaVinci Resolve — a different kind of problem-solving, but the same instinct for getting the details right.
