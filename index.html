01 — about

Practical software, built to work.

I’m a B.Tech Computer Science student at Government Engineering College, Jhalawar, currently in the 2023–2027 batch. Most of what I build starts as a genuine problem I want to solve — not a tutorial exercise — which is why I gravitate toward complete, deployable applications rather than half-finished demos.

My focus sits across Python development, web development, and software engineering more broadly — from backend APIs and databases to the interfaces people actually interact with. I enjoy the full loop: figuring out the right architecture, writing the code, fixing the inevitable bugs, and getting something real into someone’s hands.

I’m equally interested in UI/UX and automation — making tools feel considered, and making repetitive work disappear. New technologies, frameworks, and problem spaces are what keep the work interesting, and I try to stay a beginner often enough to keep learning.
